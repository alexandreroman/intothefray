# Galaxy's Most Wanted scenarios

- The five scenarios of The Galaxy's Most Wanted (`gmw`) are curated:
  Brotherhood of Badoon (Drang), Infiltrate the Museum and Escape the
  Museum (Collector), Nebula and Ronan the Accuser. Each starts on
  stage I in Standard and stage II in Expert; Escape the Museum has one
  double-sided villain card per difficulty (Collector A1 16080, B1
  16081). Main schemes start on their first card, face 0. None is
  self-contained; the modular sets come from Cerebro.
- Required sets (`sets`): Ship Command for Badoon, Nebula and Ronan;
  Galactic Artifacts for both Collector scenarios; Power Stone for
  Nebula and Ronan.
- In play at setup: each scenario's environment (Badoon Ship, Library
  Labyrinth, Nebula's Ship, Kree Command Ship), the Milano (16142) and
  the Power Stone (16149) through their sets' `inPlay` (Setup keyword),
  and Ronan's Universal Weapon (16109) laid out `with` his stage. All of
  them lie in the villain row: players attach them, and hand the Milano
  and Ronan's Power Stone to the first player, by hand.
- Escape the Museum sets aside the whole Ship Command set (16142–16148)
  on the scenario's set-aside pile; the lobby does not offer Ship
  Command there (see [[project_scenario_required_sets]]).
- Infiltrate the Museum has a curated out-of-play area, The Collection
  (see [[project_scenario_areas]]).
- The Market, Challenge and Badoon Headhunter are campaign-only sets:
  `unlisted` in `sets.json`. The Market's cards carry the player back
  (see [[project_campaign_card_backs]]).
- Manual: the cards the Setup texts move from the decks (the top card
  of each player's deck into The Collection, Nebula's discarded
  Techniques), the Milano and the rest of Ship Command entering in
  Escape the Museum's stages 2A and 3A, every counter (barrage,
  evasion, fury, poison, growth, charge) through the generic token.
  Campaign mode is out of scope.

**Why:** user decision: as little automation as possible; Setup-keyword
cards and the cards a printed Setup puts in play are placed, searches
stay manual, as in [[project_red_skull_scenarios]].

**How to apply:** curate further boxes the same way, checking each 1A
"Contents" and "Setup" line against the rulebook. See
[[project_setup_scope]], [[project_set_setup]] and
[[project_scenario_decks]].
