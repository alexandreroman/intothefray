# Mad Titan's Shadow scenarios

- The five scenarios of The Mad Titan's Shadow (`mts`) are curated: Ebony
  Maw, Tower Defense (Proxima Midnight and Corvus Glaive), Thanos, Hela
  and Loki. Each starts on stage I in Standard and stage II in Expert,
  its main schemes on their first card, face 0; Hela has one
  double-sided villain card per difficulty (Hela A 21136, Hela B 21137).
  None is self-contained; the modular sets come from Cerebro.
- Tower Defense lays out four stages: Proxima Midnight, her scheme Under
  Siege (21098), Corvus Glaive, his scheme The Armies of Thanos (21099,
  on 2A, as the 1A Setup reveals it). Focused Defense (21101) lies by
  Corvus's scheme and the Avengers Tower (21100) on its stronghold side,
  from 2A's When Revealed. No active counter: Focused Defense names the
  active villain.
- Thanos and Loki require the Infinity Gauntlet set (`sets`). Its
  Gauntlet (21129, Setup keyword) starts in the villain row through the
  set's `inPlay`, for the players to attach; the six stones (21130–21135)
  form the shuffled "Infinity Stone" scenario deck, not an encounter
  deck, with its own discard pile. The set is `unlisted`.
- Hela starts with captive Odin (21139, face 0) laid out `with` the main
  scheme, Gnipahellir and Garm in play, and sets aside Gjallerbru,
  Skurge, Hall of Nastrond and Nidhogg. Garm lies in the villain row;
  players engage it with the first player by hand.
- Loki's villain stage holds the five Loki versions and starts on 21160
  at both difficulties; War in Asgard starts in play. Players pick the
  random starting Loki and every later swap with "Change card…", and
  count the defeated Lokis themselves.
- Manual: Loki's random pick, revealing the top stone (Loki's Setup,
  Thanos's 1B), the Black Order Besiegers and Sanctuary searched for,
  Ebony Maw's Spell cards, threat and counters.
- Mad Titan's Shadow campaign (21180–21193) is a campaign-only set,
  `unlisted` (see [[project_set_setup]]). Spectrum's energy forms start in
  play (see [[project_hero_side_decks]]).

**Why:** user decisions: as little automation as possible, as in
[[project_galaxys_most_wanted_scenarios]]; the cards a printed Setup puts
in play are placed, a minion it reveals included; a random choice among
stage cards stays manual, as Absorbing Man's random environment does.

**How to apply:** curate further boxes the same way, checking each 1A
"Contents" and "Setup" line against the rulebook. See
[[project_setup_scope]], [[project_scenario_decks]] and
[[project_scenario_required_sets]].
